# Reverso video — three storylines

Assumptions: 2:30–2:50, 1920×1080, English voiceover over music, product shots are real captures of the app.

## A. Maria's phone (recommended: a stranger follows a person, not a diagram)
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take that dives into the phone and comes back out.
We watch one phone drown: calls, texts and car ads stack up after a single form. One tap turns the screen upside down, the noise falls away, and the same phone shows dealers bidding for her.
- First two seconds: a phone lock screen at 6:42 pm, the first "Missed call" banner dropping in while two more arrive.
- Beats: phone floods → "Come in and we'll talk" → the form's data flying to dealers → screen rotates 180° into Reverso → real chat, sealed offers, reveal → pull back to the dealer's live board → fees land, one refund flies back → end card.
- Look: white ground, Geist, the app's blue-to-green gradient only on the flip, the best offer and the end card.
- Sound: buzzes and notification pings pile up to a hard cut of silence on the flip, then one calm, steady track.

## B. The flip
Device: type carries the argument. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
A market diagram draws itself: one buyer in the middle, arrows from every dealer pointing at her. The word LEAD gets chased across the frame. Then every arrow reverses and the same diagram becomes the product.
- First two seconds: the word PRICE? huge on white, struck by the first incoming arrow.
- Beats: arrows multiply → "her contact is the product" → all arrows reverse → dealers become sealed cards → real UI as proof → three fees stack as bars, a refund bar retracts → end card.
- Look: white, black type at poster scale, arrows and one gradient.
- Sound: percussive cuts on the beat.

## C. The sealed offer (the bold one)
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: match cuts on one shape.
We follow a single sealed offer, a card with a blurred name, from a dealer's desk through the auction to Maria. The blur is the recurring shape; it only clears at the payoff. Then the camera follows the money back the other way.
- First two seconds: a blurred dealer name over a sharp price, a lock clicking shut.
- Beats: the card is written → joins three others → sorted by real cost, one sinks → Maria taps → blur clears → $40, $75, $150 travel back along the same path → one refund returns → end card.
- Look: soft depth, cards on white, blur as the only effect.
- Sound: quiet, close, a lock and a release.
