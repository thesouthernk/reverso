# Reverso — demo video voiceover (English, ~300 words, target 2:30–2:50)

The hero is the buyer. Prices and fees are sample data from the demo.

## 1. The ordeal (0:00–0:24)
Maria wants one thing: the price of a new SUV.
So she fills out one form.
By dinner she has eleven missed calls. Four dealerships have her number. Every app she opens is now selling her a car.
And nobody has told her the price. "Come in and we'll talk."

## 2. Why it works this way (0:24–0:38)
Because her request became a product. Quote sites sell the same form as a lead to several dealers at once, and dealers pay for ads that follow her across every app. They all paid for her before she decided anything, so they all chase her.

## 3. The flip (0:36–0:46)
Reverso turns the market around. Dealers compete for Maria, and they don't learn who she is until she picks one.

## 4. Her side, in the real product (0:46–1:45)
She tells the agent what she wants. It asks what a good salesperson would ask: where she is, how she's paying, when she's buying.
She confirms it's really her with PayPal.
Then the request goes out with no name and no number.
Dealers' agents answer with sealed, out-the-door prices.
Maria's agent reads the fine print. This quote looks cheapest, but it leaves out a mandatory package and only holds with dealer financing. It drops to last.
She picks the best one. Only now does that one dealer get her contact.
She sees the exact car. The price is held for thirty minutes, and she can reserve it with a refundable deposit through PayPal, or book a visit.

## 5. The dealer's side (1:45–2:22)
For the dealer, this replaces buying ads and cold leads. Requests arrive live and already qualified: budget, payment, timing, verified identity. Their agent bids inside a floor they set.
They pay for what happened. A contact costs forty dollars. A booked visit, seventy-five. A paid reservation, one hundred fifty.
If the buyer never answers, or the sale falls through, PayPal sends the money back automatically.

## 6. Return (2:22–2:40)
Maria got the real price and kept her privacy. The dealer paid only when a buyer chose them.
Reverso. The car market, the right way round.
